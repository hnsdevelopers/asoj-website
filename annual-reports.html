Annual Report 2025-26

Mahila Panchayat Programme supported by Azim Premji Foundation, Digital Learning Centre achievements, child-protection initiatives, and women's-empowerment success stories.

  • 250+ domestic violence cases registered, 102 resolved
  • 22,891 households contacted door-to-door
  • 55 students completed the NIIT Foundation CCAB course
  • 2,040+ women took part in nukad meetings

Annual Report 2024-25

Mahila Panchayat Programme supported by the Delhi Commission for Women, health camps, school-enrolment drives, and community-awareness initiatives.

  • 48 nukad meetings with 2,430 women
  • 4,050 people benefited from health camps
  • 65 out-of-school children enrolled in school
  • 270 people obtained Aadhaar/PAN cards

Annual Report 2023-24

Continued Mahila Panchayat casework, health-camp delivery, and school-enrolment support across Ghazipur and Gharoli.

  • 47 Mahila Panchayat meetings organised
  • Health camps and document-support camps continued
  • Ongoing remedial-education support at both CAC centres

Annual Report 2022-23

Child Activity Centres operations, digital-learning initiatives, COVID-19 relief work, and community-mobilisation efforts.

  • 185 children received remedial education at CAC
  • 50 nukad meetings with 2,100 women
  • 143 out-of-school children enrolled in schools
  • 55 children completed the CCAB course

Annual Report 2021-22

COVID-19 response, vaccination-awareness campaigns, school-reopening support, and community-health initiatives during the pandemic.

  • 173 domestic violence cases reported, 127 resolved
  • 90% adult vaccination rate in target areas
  • 77 out-of-school children enrolled in school
  • 3,000+ sanitary pads distributed
