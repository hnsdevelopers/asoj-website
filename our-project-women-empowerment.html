Women Empowerment Programme

Building self-reliant, confident and empowered women.

Programme overview

The Women Empowerment programme creates self-reliant, confident and economically independent women through legal awareness, skill development, social-security linkage and leadership training.

Through the Mahila Panchayat network, we have linked hundreds of women to government schemes, opened bank accounts, provided skills training, and created platforms for women to voice their concerns and claim their rights.

Social-security scheme linkages

Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana

40 girls linked to this savings scheme for education and marriage costs.

Ladli Yojana

16 girls associated with the Delhi Government's Ladli scheme.

Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana

30 women received free LPG connections.

Widow pension scheme

139 widows now receive ₹2,500 per month.

Old-age pension scheme

224 senior citizens now receive ₹2,000–₹2,500 monthly.

Disabled pension scheme

49 people with disabilities now receive ₹2,500 monthly.

Financial inclusion

Bank account camp

45 women opened accounts with Bank of Baroda.

Aadhaar & PAN support

270 people helped to obtain Aadhaar/PAN cards through special camps.

Gender sensitisation & awareness

Two workshops with 40 women each covered gender discrimination, social norms, and patriarchal structures, using poster-making, slogan-writing and nukkad plays. Two mega meetings with Delhi Commission for Women volunteers reached 210 women, covering workplace sexual harassment and legal reporting mechanisms.

Women's helpline & crisis support

Door-to-door awareness was conducted about the Women's Helpline Number 181, and about the One Stop Centre in Kalyanpuri, which offers legal assistance, counselling, and shelter (including a facility to stay 5–7 days with children).
