What drives us

Our Vision & Mission

Working towards a just and equitable society, one household and one case at a time.

Home / Vision & Mission

Our vision

Formation of an equitable society where children, women and other marginalised people can live with self-esteem and dignity — empowered through education, awareness generation and capacity building.

Our mission

To promote social justice through research, advocacy and community engagement by addressing systemic inequalities and injustices in society.

  • Restoration of education, health and protection rights for children
  • Strong grassroots advocacy for gender equality
  • Gender-perspective building through constant dialogue
  • Empowering marginalised communities through education

Goals & objectives

  • Restoration of education, health and protection rights for children
  • Grassroots advocacy for gender equality, enabling women to become self-reliant and live a healthy, dignified life
  • Gender-perspective building and attitudinal change through dialogue, communication and information
  • Empowering children and women to organise themselves and articulate inequality
  • Linking marginalised communities to the education system
  • Making systems accessible for marginalised communities through leadership development and awareness

Our core values

Social justice

Committed to addressing systemic inequalities and promoting fairness for all.

Equity & inclusion

Ensuring equal opportunities regardless of gender, caste or background.

Research-based approach

Using rigorous research and data to inform programmes and advocacy.

Community engagement

Working collaboratively with communities to create sustainable change.
