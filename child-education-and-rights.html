Child Education & Rights

Helping children from marginalised communities in East Delhi stay in school, catch up academically, and stay safe.

Home / Child Education & Rights

Restoring education, health and protection rights for children is one of ASOJ's founding goals. Below is a brief look at how that work runs today across our Child Activity Centres, our Digital Learning Centre, and our child-protection efforts. A dedicated page for this area is still being built — in the meantime, here's the short version.

Child Activity Centres (CAC)

Two centres — in Ghazipur slum and Gharoli — give 160+ children from classes 2–10 remedial education support after school hours, helping first-generation learners keep pace and stay enrolled.

  • 143 out-of-school children enrolled in mainstream schools through our advocacy
  • 4 children secured top positions in their classes
  • Support for children denied school admission due to missing documents

Digital Learning Centre

Affiliated with NIIT Foundation, our Digital Learning Centre runs a CCAB-certified computer course for youth aged 13–18, bridging the digital divide for young people who would otherwise have no access to computer education.

  • 55 students enrolled annually, 100% course-completion rate
  • Weekly English-grammar sessions run by Cognizant volunteers
  • Graduates have gone on to further courses such as COPA and web design

Child Protection

We run good-touch/bad-touch and POCSO awareness sessions in schools and communities, and support legal follow-through when children are harmed.
