About Association for Social Justice and Research

A grassroots, research-driven non-profit working alongside women, children and families in East Delhi's under-served settlements.

Home / About Us

Who we are

Association for Social Justice and Research (ASOJ) is a national-level registered society formed by like-minded activists and researchers dedicated to promoting social justice through research, advocacy and community engagement. Since our registration on 23 December 2009, we have worked to address systemic inequalities affecting women and children in East Delhi's informal settlements.

We operate with the active support of volunteers and a governing body that guides the planning and implementation of our work. Our approach combines grassroots outreach, legal awareness, case counselling and partnerships with government bodies and larger NGOs — because, in our experience, grassroots work alone rarely produces lasting change without sustained engagement with the systems around it.

Our mission

To promote social justice through research, advocacy and community engagement — restoring education, health and protection rights for children, and enabling women to become self-reliant and live with dignity.

Our vision

An equitable society where children, women and other marginalised people can live with self-esteem and dignity, empowered through education, awareness and capacity building.

Our journey

  • 2009

    Foundation of ASOJ

    Registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860 on 23 December 2009 in New Delhi, by a group of activists and intellectuals committed to social justice.

  • 2013

    Mahila Panchayat Programme launched

    Started at Ghazipur Dairy Farm with support from the Delhi Commission for Women — an alternative justice delivery system bringing legal aid and dispute resolution to women's doorsteps.

  • 2020–21

    CRY partnership begins

    Partnered with Child Rights and You (CRY) to focus on child rights, education and protection in the Ghazipur and Gharoli areas, reaching thousands of out-of-school children.

  • 2021–22

    Community support during COVID-19

    Distributed thermal scanners, oximeters, masks and sanitisers; organised vaccination camps and reached over 10,000 community members with awareness campaigns.

  • 2022–23

    Digital Learning Centre established

    Launched in affiliation with NIIT Foundation, providing CCAB-certified computer education to 55 youths annually and bridging the digital divide.

  • 2025–26

    Azim Premji Foundation support

    The Mahila Panchayat Programme began receiving support from Azim Premji Foundation, expanding reach to roughly 4,000 families across Ghazipur and Gharoli.

What we do

Mahila Panchayat Programme

  • 250+ domestic violence cases registered
  • 102 cases successfully resolved through counselling
  • 22,891 households reached through door-to-door outreach
  • 80+ nukad meetings with 2,040+ women participants

Child Activity Centres (CAC)

  • 2 centres operating in Ghazipur and Gharoli
  • 160+ children receiving remedial education
  • 143 out-of-school children enrolled in mainstream schools
  • 4 children secured top positions in their classes

Digital Learning Centre

  • 55 students enrolled in the CCAB-certified course annually
  • 100% course completion rate
  • Weekly English-grammar sessions run by Cognizant volunteers
  • Career counselling and mentoring support

Health & WASH Programme

  • 4,050+ beneficiaries reached at health camps
  • 300+ Abha health cards created
  • 90% adult vaccination rate in target areas
  • Monthly sanitary-pad distribution with PINKISHE Foundation

Our core values

Social justice

Addressing systemic inequalities and promoting fairness, equity and dignity for all, regardless of gender, caste, religion or background.

Research-based approach

Using rigorous research and data collection to design programmes, measure impact and inform advocacy.

Community engagement

Working alongside communities as equal partners and building solutions from within, not imposing them from outside.

Transparency & accountability

Maintaining high standards of financial transparency and regulatory compliance to donors, partners and the communities we serve.
