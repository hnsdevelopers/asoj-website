Case Studies

First-hand accounts of what happens when a woman in East Delhi asks the Mahila Panchayat for help.

Home / Case Studies
Names used here are first names only, to protect the identity of the women we work with. Each case is drawn from our Mahila Panchayat programme records.
Domestic Violence & Mediation

Radha

Mulla Colony, Delhi
Challenge
Faced domestic violence, triple talaq attempt, and eviction. Later diagnosed with a brain tumor while fighting for her rights.
Action taken
Mahila Panchayat conducted counseling sessions with her husband, provided medical guidance, and facilitated mediation hearings.
Outcome
Her husband agreed to reunite with Radha, take financial responsibility, and support her medical treatment.
Domestic Violence & Mediation

Sunita

Gharoli Sapera Basti, Delhi
Challenge
Continuous domestic violence from her husband leaving visible injury marks; severe emotional and psychological distress.
Action taken
Persistent counseling, home visits, and mediation even when husband initially refused to attend hearings.
Outcome
Sunita and her husband are now living together with significant positive improvement in their relationship.
Addiction Recovery

Kiran

Shyam Lal Chowk, Gharoli, Delhi
Challenge
Husband addicted to alcohol — abused her, sold household items, and often forced her and children out of the house.
Action taken
Repeated counseling and home visits; husband was eventually admitted to a de-addiction center for 6-7 months.
Outcome
Husband returned sober, apologized, and now contributes to household expenses in a peaceful family environment.
