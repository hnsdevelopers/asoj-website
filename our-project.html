Child Activity Centres, Digital Learning & Child Protection

Child Activity Centres (CAC)

Two centres in Ghazipur and Gharoli give 160+ children from classes 2–10 remedial support after school hours — helping first-generation learners keep pace and stay enrolled.

  • 143 out-of-school children enrolled in mainstream schools
  • 65 out-of-school children enrolled in a single recent year
  • 4 children secured top positions in their classes

Digital Learning Centre

Affiliated with NIIT Foundation, our Digital Learning Centre runs a CCAB-certified computer course for youth aged 13–18.

  • 55 students enrolled annually, 100% completion rate
  • Weekly English-grammar sessions by Cognizant volunteers
  • Graduates have gone on to COPA and web-design courses

Child Protection

We run good-touch/bad-touch and POCSO awareness sessions in schools and communities, and support legal follow-through when children are harmed.
